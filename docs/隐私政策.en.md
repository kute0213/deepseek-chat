# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DSW (DeepSeekWebAPIChat) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Please read it before using the Service.

## 1. Information we collect

1. **Account data**: username, password hash, registration time, role, and status. Passwords are stored only as hashes; the operator cannot see plain-text passwords.
2. **Conversation data**: session titles, message content, model settings, and timestamps.
3. **Attachments**: the images and files you upload, plus metadata such as name, type, and size.
4. **API key**: the key you provide for DeepSeek API calls. To let you continue from other devices, the key is stored on the server (plain text or encrypted, depending on configuration) and used only to call the model.
5. **Share data**: links you create, the snapshot of selected messages, view counts, and creation time.
6. **Basic logs**: the server may keep necessary logs of sign-ins and admin actions for security and troubleshooting.

## 2. How we use information

1. To provide core features: chat, attachment processing, history, search, sharing, and export.
2. For authentication, security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.
3. We do not use your conversations to train models, and we do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Third-party services

1. Chat responses rely on the DeepSeek API. The messages you send are transmitted to that service to generate replies, subject to its privacy policy.
2. We do not disclose your information to other third parties except as required to provide the Service.

## 4. Storage and protection

1. Data is stored on servers and databases deployed by the site operator.
2. We use access control, password hashing, and API authentication to protect data, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
3. Do not submit highly sensitive information such as ID or bank card numbers in conversations.

## 5. Your rights

1. **Access and export**: export all sessions and attachments under Settings → Data.
2. **Correction**: change your username and password at any time.
3. **Deletion**: delete individual sessions, all sessions, or your account. Account deletion removes related data permanently.
4. **Share management**: view and revoke all share links you created. Revocation takes effect immediately.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

1. The Service uses cookies or equivalent tokens to keep you signed in.
2. The front end stores preferences such as theme, language, and drafts locally in your browser. You can remove them by clearing browser data.

## 7. Minors

Minors should use the Service under the guidance of a guardian, who is responsible for supervising such use.

## 8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the Service evolves and will be published on this page. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how personal information is handled, contact us through the channel published by the site operator.
